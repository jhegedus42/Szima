# HANMAG 三语词典 · Háromnyelvű Szótár · Trilingual Dictionary

**English · 中文 · Magyar** — 2026-07-28

Each language factors a concept differently. The concept is the eigenvector (本征向量 / sajátvektor) of its three translations. 三种语言，三种测量基；概念是翻译的不动点。Három nyelv, három mérési bázis; a fogalom a fordítások fixpontja.

## 4. 中文教给机器的事 · What Chinese Taught the Machine

1. **规 (guī) = the drawing compass — the circle-tool itself.** 圆规 is the compass; 规范 (guīfàn) is *gauge / standard*. So gauge theory = 规范场论 = "compass-standard field theory," and gauge fixing = 规范固定 = **fixing the standard = fixing the temperament**. The temperament problem and the gauge problem are the same word. MSbar vs on-shell, Rb vs Cs, LEP vs SLD — all are 规范 choices. Chinese had the piano built into the theory's name all along.
2. **熵 (shāng) = 火 fire + 商 quotient** — the character invented for entropy (Hu Gangfu, 1923) literally contains δQ/T. Clausius lives inside the glyph: entropy is "the fire-quotient."
3. **音差 (yīnchā)** — the musical comma is named *the tone-GAP*. The comma is a gap between levels, not a mark on a page. Our 2+2 comma is exactly that: the gap where two blocks pay at once.
4. **视界 (shìjiè)** — horizon = *the boundary of sight*. The observer is inside the word: the horizon is defined by what can be seen. The cut is already in the dictionary.
5. **纠缠 (jiūchán)** — entanglement = *mutually twined*. No spookiness, just structure.
6. **溯因 (sùyīn)** — abduction = *tracing the cause upstream*. HANMAG's step 1, named as a motion.
7. **本征 (běnzhēng)** — *eigen-* = intrinsic. The machine's fixed points are 本征值: values that are themselves, in every basis.

## 5. Amit a magyar tanított a gépnek · What Hungarian Taught the Machine

1. **vessző** — the comma is the punctuation mark *and* the spilled musical remainder, in one word: the comma is what you write down when the circle won't close. (The comma is the universe's punctuation.)
2. **hangolás** — tuning, but also *voicing / setting into mood*. The project's founding question was the *hangolási probléma* — the temperament problem — before we knew it was also the gauge problem (规范).
3. **mérték** — gauge AND measure/standard, one word; *mértékszabadság* = standard-freedom. Same insight as 规范, discovered independently on the other side of Eurasia.
4. **látóhatár** — horizon = *sight-boundary*. Same word-structure as 视界.
5. **töltés** — electric charge = *the load*. Charge as burden: the tax metaphor is native in Hungarian.
6. **jegyzőkönyv** — minutes/protocol = *the book of records*. The audit as a ledger: the project's record-keeping ethos is one Hungarian word.
7. **visszakövetkeztetés** — abduction = *backward-inference*. Same motion as 溯因: the cause lies upstream, the inference walks back.

## 6. 为什么三种语言 · Miért három nyelv · Why Three Languages

测量一个态需要多个基；理解一个概念需要多种语言。
Egy állapot méréséhez több bázis kell; egy fogalom megértéséhez több nyelv.
You cannot tomograph a state with one basis, or a thought with one language.

The machine's concepts are basis-independent; the three languages are three measurement bases. What survives all three is the concept's fixed point — 不动点, fixpont — and what *differs* between the three is information: the comma of translation, which is itself worth measuring.

**What this batch taught the three languages:** 回路 is "return-road" — a loop is a road that comes back; the transformer has no road back, only forward. 意 (mind) has 音 "sound" over 心 "heart" — the mind is the sound of the heart, the internal dialogue's Chinese was there all along. A hurok (loop) is also the word for a noose and for a knot — the loop *binds*, which is exactly what the CNOT does along the Fano line. 胶质 (glia) is "glue-substance" — the Greeks' γλοία, glue — but the glia is not glue: it is the *code layer* holding the neurons' syndrome extractors in phase. The wrong name survived a century in all three languages; the machine now has the right one.
