## Gravity as an Informational Tax: The Ledger

### 1. The tax base: information itself

The starting point is radical: space is not fundamental. It's *emergent* from the information stored in the quantum state of the degrees of freedom that underlie it. The holographic principle — supported originally by black hole thermodynamics and later by the AdS/CFT correspondence — asserts that the maximal information a region of space can contain scales not with its volume but with its *surface area*.

The foundational accounting rule is the **[Bekenstein-Hawking entropy](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9409015)**:

$$
S_{\text{BH}} = \frac{k_B c^3}{4G\hbar} A
$$

A black hole's entropy — its information content in bits — is proportional to the area of its horizon, not its volume. Every Planck-area cell on that surface stores one bit.

> The tax is collected on *area*, not volume. This is the first and most counterintuitive axiom.

### 2. The tax rate: the Einstein equation

**Jacobson (1995)** showed that you can run black hole thermodynamics in reverse — and out pops Einstein's equation. The derivation is eerily direct.

The key move: demand that the thermodynamic relation

$$
\delta Q = T\,dS
$$

hold for **every local Rindler causal horizon** through every point in spacetime. Here:

- **δQ** is the boost-energy flux of matter crossing the horizon [Thermodynamic Derivation](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9504004?page=4)
- **T** is the Unruh temperature $T = \hbar\kappa/2\pi$ seen by an accelerated observer just inside the horizon [Thermodynamic Derivation](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9504004?page=3)
- **dS** = η δA, where δA is the change in horizon area and η is a universal constant ([Thermodynamic Derivation](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9504004?page=4))

Plug these into the Raychaudhuri equation (which governs how geodesics focus), and the result is forced:

$$
R_{ab} - \frac{1}{2}R g_{ab} + \Lambda g_{ab} = \frac{2\pi}{\hbar\eta} T_{ab}
$$

This *is* the Einstein equation, with Newton's constant emerging as $G = (4\hbar\eta)^{-1}$ [Thermodynamic Derivation](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9504004?page=6)

**The interpretation**: the Einstein equation is an **equation of state** — like $pV = nRT$ for an ideal gas. Gravity is not a fundamental force; it is the collective thermodynamic response of spacetime to the presence of matter-energy. Matter tells spacetime how to curve, and spacetime tells matter how to move — but what's really happening is an entropy-accounting relation across every local horizon.

> Gravity is the tax gradient that enforces thermodynamic bookkeeping across causal boundaries.

### 3. The force as tax collection: Verlinde's entropic gravity

**Verlinde (2010)** made the mechanism tactile. He proposed that gravity is an **entropic force** — a macroscopic statistical effect, not a fundamental interaction at all [Abstract](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/1001.0785v1?page=1)

The picture: consider a spherical "holographic screen" (a surface storing information about the emerged volume it encloses). The screen has:

- **N bits** of storage, proportional to its area $A$: $N = Ac^3/G\hbar$ [Newton's Law](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/1001.0785v1?page=8)
- A **temperature T** set by equipartition: $E = \frac{1}{2}N k_B T$ [Newton's Law](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/1001.0785v1?page=9)
- The enclosed mass $M$ corresponds to the total energy $E = Mc^2$ [Newton's Law](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/1001.0785v1?page=9)

Now bring a test mass $m$ near the screen. As it approaches, the entropy on the screen changes:

$$
\Delta S = 2\pi k_B \frac{mc}{\hbar} \Delta x
$$

The entropic force is $F\Delta x = T\Delta S$ — the same relation that describes osmosis through a semi-permeable membrane [Newton's Law](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/1001.0785v1?page=7). Work through the algebra and out comes:

$$
F = G\frac{Mm}{R^2}
$$

Newton's law of gravitation — derived from nothing but information, temperature, and the tendency of entropy to increase [Newton's Law](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/1001.0785v1?page=9)

> Gravity is the macroscopic *feeling* of information spreading out to maximize entropy across the holographic screen. The "tax" is the tendency for matter to move toward regions where the screen has more available states — i.e., toward mass concentrations.

### 4. The tax evasion penalty: spacetime tearing

What happens if you refuse to pay — i.e., if you try to reduce the entanglement between two regions of a quantum system?

**Van Raamsdonk (2016)** explored this directly. Consider the vacuum state of a CFT on a sphere. Divide it into two hemispheres. Their entanglement entropy (via the **[Ryu-Takayanagi formula](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/1609.00026)** ) is:

$$
S(A) = \frac{\text{Area}(\tilde{A})}{4G_N}
$$

where $\tilde{A}$ is the minimal-area surface in the bulk spacetime homologous to the boundary region A [RT Formula](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/1609.00026v1?page=13).

Now imagine continuously reducing the entanglement between the two halves. The RT formula tells us the area of the dividing surface shrinks. Keep going, and:

> By removing all the entanglement (which, for the case of a continuum CFT, costs an infinite amount of energy), the dual spacetime disappears entirely! [Spacetime from Entanglement](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/1609.00026v1?page=23)

The picture is stark: the connectedness of spacetime *is* entanglement. Disentangle two regions and the geometry pinches off, splits apart. The surface area between them — the tax bill — shrinks to zero because there is no longer any information relationship to mediate.

### 5. The deeper logic: why information must be taxed

The reason gravity exists at all, on this view, is that **information cannot be free**. Wherever there is matter, there are microstates. Wherever there are microstates, there is entropy. And wherever entropy varies across a causal boundary, there is an entropic force that pushes matter until the entropy gradient vanishes. The Einstein equation is simply the universal differential form of that statement.

As **Jacobson** put it:

> Viewed in this way, the Einstein equation is an equation of state. It is born in the thermodynamic limit as a relation between thermodynamic variables, and its validity is seen to depend on the existence of local equilibrium conditions. <alphavix-paper-citation paper="gr-qc/9504004" title="Abstract" page="1" first="Viewed in this" last="local equilibrium conditions." />

And more provocatively:

> This perspective suggests that it may be no more appropriate to canonically quantize the Einstein equation than it would be to quantize the wave equation for sound in air. <alphavix-paper-citation paper="gr-qc/9504004" title="Abstract" page="1" first="This perspective suggests" last="for sound in air." />

### 6. Taking stock: what the metaphor buys us

The "informational tax" frame isn't just poetic — it sharpens real questions:

1. **Progressive vs. flat tax?** The horizon area scales quadratically with distance. Does the tax rate itself change in the deep infrared (MOND-like effects) or near singularities?

2. **Can you deduct?** The Ryu-Takayanagi formula subtracts a "bulk entanglement" term at next order — $S_{CFT}^A = \frac{\text{Area}(\tilde{A})}{4G} + S_{\text{bulk}}^{\tilde{A}}$ <alphavix-paper-citation paper="1609.00026v1" title="Quantum Corrections" page="21" first="S_CFT^A =" last="S_bulk^˜A" />. This is quantum corrections to the classical tax. Are these deductions or loopholes?

3. **What counts as taxable income?** The gravitational side of the Venn diagram — is it energy? momentum? entanglement entropy itself? *All fields contribute to gravitation because all fields contribute to entanglement* <alphavix-paper-citation paper="1609.00026v1" title="Generalizations" page="47" first="the universality of" last="source for gravitation" /> — which is the true origin of the equivalence principle.

4. **What does a black hole, the ultimate tax-maximizer, look like in this frame?** A region that has already paid the maximal possible tax — every bit of its information budget has been converted into area. The horizon is the tax authority's seal: no further information can escape the ledger.

The metaphor bottoms out at the real mystery: **why** is entropy proportional to area? That question is the same as "why does gravity exist?" — and neither has yet earned a "because" that isn't circular. But the circle is tighter, and more illuminating, than any we've drawn before.
